Need a bed tonight? Call the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 1-877-424-3838 or dial 211. Both are free, 24/7, and will find the nearest open shelter.

Find Shelters & Housing Near You

Enter your ZIP code, or your city and state, to find emergency shelters, transitional housing, and VA housing programs in your area.

Types of Housing Help

🏠 Emergency & Transitional Shelter
Emergency shelters give you a safe bed tonight. Transitional housing gives veterans up to 24 months of housing plus support to get back on their feet (the VA Grant & Per Diem program funds many beds reserved for veterans).
HUD Find Shelter →VA Grant & Per Diem →
🔑 Housing Vouchers & Rapid Re-Housing
HUD-VASH combines a Section 8 housing voucher with VA case management. SSVF offers rapid re-housing and help preventing eviction. Both are veteran-specific and free.
HUD-VASH & SSVF →Prevent eviction (SSVF) →

VA Housing Programs

🎖️ Start With the VA
If you are a veteran at risk of or experiencing homelessness, the VA has dedicated case managers. Call 1-877-4AID-VET any time, or use the locator to find VA homeless services near you.
