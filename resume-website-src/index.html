Before you begin

A few helpful answers.

Make your next step an informed one.

Can I use a resume I already have?

Yes. Upload a PDF, Word (.docx) or TXT file up to 10 MB, or paste your resume. You can also use it to update your profile, reviewing and confirming each section before it is saved.

Can I include military and civilian experience?

Yes. Include military service, civilian or government jobs, education, certifications, training, skills, languages and projects. The service library helps you identify specialties and qualifications from your own records.

Does this support federal applications?

Yes. Federal layouts include details such as month and year employment dates and hours per week, with a two-page PDF length check. Always follow the specific job announcement and its requirements.

Do I need a cover letter or Virtual CV?

No. Both are separate services in your workspace. Create a cover letter when you need an introduction for a particular role, or publish a Virtual CV when you want a professional profile you can share.

Where do I find my finished resume?

Open your workspace, sign in with the same account and choose My resumes. You can return to saved documents and download them again. Purchase history also helps you recover a paid attempt.

How is my information handled?

Your account documents use protected storage, and you decide what to share on a Virtual CV. Optional analytics excludes resume text and personal fields. Read our privacy and AI notice.

Can I add Veteran Resumes to my home screen?

Yes. Choose Add shortcut and follow your browser’s instructions to add Veteran Resumes to your phone or computer.
