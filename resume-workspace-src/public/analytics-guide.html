Start with Realtime

  1. Sign in to Google Analytics with the Google account that manages Veteran Career Path. Select the VeteranResumes.com property containing destination G-H7B54FKZGX.
  2. Open the resume app in a normal browser and choose Allow usage analytics. Visit Home and My resumes, or start a resume.
  3. In Analytics, open Reports → Realtime. Look for page titles starting with “Veteran Resumes” and event names starting with vcp_resume_.

Realtime appears before standard reports. Browser privacy signals, ad blockers, a declined analytics choice and a blocked measurement connection can prevent a test visit from appearing. These do not prevent use of the app.

The Resume Funnel

Create a Funnel exploration with these events in order. This shows where consenting visitors stop, so you can improve the experience.

  1. vcp_resume_start — chooses Upload or Build manually
  2. vcp_resume_checkout_requested — requests checkout after accepting the purchase terms
  3. vcp_resume_checkout_verified — returns from checkout and the server verifies the purchase
  4. vcp_resume_review_started — asks the app to review the resume
  5. vcp_resume_review_complete — receives a readable completed review
  6. vcp_resume_download — the app starts a PDF, Word or TXT download

Mark vcp_resume_checkout_verified and vcp_resume_download as key events in Google Analytics if you want them in conversion reports.

Use Stripe for revenue. A verified checkout includes a successful $0 promo checkout. Analytics does not send card details, coupon codes, purchase IDs or revenue amounts. It can miss returns and downloads because of consent, blockers or closed tabs; it is not a payment ledger or an exact count of all customers.

Find Usage and Friction

  • page_view: general app screens; filter by the fixed app paths above.
  • vcp_resume_import_complete / vcp_resume_import_failed: local imports that finish or fail. File type may be PDF, DOCX, TXT or other; filenames are excluded.
  • vcp_resume_review_failed: a general timeout, connection, cancellation, provider or other category. Error text and submitted data are excluded.
  • vcp_resume_active: periodic activity on visible ordinary app screens. It supports general engagement reporting.

Optional event parameters such as path, file_type, kind, reason and format can be registered as event-scoped custom dimensions in Analytics if you want detailed breakdowns. Basic page/event counts work without registering them.

Visitor Privacy and Reporting Limits

Analytics begins only after the separate app choice. It has its own random browser/session IDs, independent of sign-in and main-site analytics IDs. Counts describe consenting browsers, not verified individual people. The dedicated property starts collecting after its November 7, 2026 activation. Earlier VeteranResumes.com history remains in the Veteran Career Path property and is not moved or backfilled.

The tag runs in an isolated document and cannot inspect resume fields. Resume/job text, names, emails, filenames, photos, account IDs, full URLs and public CV links are excluded. Analytics pauses on the VA calculator and while emergency help is open; these actions and their entries are not measured. Public Virtual CV pages are not tagged by this integration. The paid app has no ads, heatmaps or session recordings. Advertising is optional and limited to the public landing page.

Visitors can choose Settings → Cookie & storage preferences → Essential only to stop measurement and clear app analytics identifiers. This does not erase Google’s previously received data or private app/purchase records. Manage Google Analytics retention, permissions and data requests in your Analytics account.
