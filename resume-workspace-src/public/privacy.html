Resume privacy & AI data notice

Effective October 24, 2026. Veteran Career Path is operated by StreamSync Solutions. This adult-only (18+) resume program supplements the Privacy Policy, program purchase terms, and Cookie Policy.

Your information and choices

Resume imports (PDF, Word .docx and text) are extracted in your browser. Original files are not uploaded by this program. Structured fields can contain your chosen contact details, military and civilian employment, education, qualifications, awards, unclassified deployments, releasable clearance details, training, skills, languages and projects. Include only information you have permission to process. Never submit SSNs, birth dates, classified information, medical records, VA claim records, passwords, or sensitive military documents.

OpenAI and Anthropic

AI services used by Veteran Career Path include OpenAI and Anthropic. The paid resume program uses the provider configured on our server: OpenAI or Anthropic. Requesting an AI build sends the fixed resume text, target role, job announcement, application type and additional verified details through our server to the configured provider. OpenAI requests set store:false. That setting is not a guarantee of zero retention. Provider API security, processing and retention terms apply; we do not promise zero data retention or that submitted data never leaves your device. Neither provider's browser tracking script is loaded by this program.

AI processing requires its own agreement. Canceling a browser request cannot recall data already sent to a provider. AI can make factual mistakes. Review every title, duty, date, number, qualification, clearance status and application requirement. We do not guarantee employment, ATS results, federal acceptance, preference or eligibility.

Guest access

You may start without an email account. Drafts stay in this browser unless you choose device storage, save to an account, or start a paid AI build. Guest checkout creates a private, temporary Firebase sign-in identity with session-only browser persistence. Purchase records, submitted build inputs, AI results and any clarification answers are stored for recovery under that identity. Guest access can be lost when the browser session ends, storage is cleared, or you sign out. Create a new email/password account from the guest session to keep that identity and its purchases. Signing in to a different existing account does not transfer guest purchases. Contact support with your transaction reference if access is lost. Never send card details.

Program accounts and optional saves

Free resume-program sign-in uses a separate named Firebase app session in the existing veteran-career-builder Firebase project. No main-site subscription or access code is required. Firebase authentication identities use the same project: if an email already exists there, sign in or reset its password. Main-site membership is not purchased, changed or required by this program.

Signing in does not automatically save your editor or structured background. Choosing “Also save the resume I submit to my account” before AI submission stores the submitted resume text, title, context and formatting settings as a saved version. This option is off by default. Other Save actions remain explicit choices. Saving a profile is authorized by choosing Save profile; optional saved resume versions, application records and edited cover letters also use the private-storage agreement. Saved profiles restore after sign-in. Program backgrounds and saved resumes use their own owner-only paths, separate from the main website profile and saved resumes. Saved editor versions include text, application context, title and formatting choices. Background saves include your structured fields. Firestore owner rules and server authorization restrict access; data is not automatically posted to a public profile. We do not enable Firestore offline disk caching.

Career profiles, applications, versions and cover letters

Career tools reuse your structured background as a career profile. Explicit Save actions store the profile, separate resume snapshots, job application records or edited cover letters in your private program account. Applications may include employer, job announcement, URL, application stage, applied/interview/follow-up dates, recruiter contact, notes and the ID/title of the resume version submitted. These dates do not schedule notifications. Only save contact details you have permission to use. Deleting a resume version does not delete a separate application record referencing its title.

The included AI career kit sends the fixed resume, verified details, job context and rewritten draft from your completed purchase to the configured OpenAI or Anthropic service. It produces requirement comparisons, service-skill translations, possible career directions and a cover letter. It requires a separate AI agreement and is saved with the private purchase for recovery. Reopening the kit does not charge again. The kit uses that purchase’s fixed job context; another target requires a new build. AI suggestions are not verified credentials, job offers, ATS scores or eligibility decisions. Cover-letter edits remain in the current tab until explicitly saved or downloaded. The app does not automatically apply to jobs or contact recruiters.

Optional resume-to-profile import

After importing a resume, you can choose to review suggested profile updates section by section. You can also import a file directly from Profile. File reading and field suggestions run on your device; this feature does not send the file or extracted text to an AI provider. Suggestions may misread formatting or miss information. Check each field and its source text, choose Add as new or an existing entry to update, and approve only accurate details. Missing facts, military skill levels, rank-based qualifications and civilian federal grades are not invented.

For signed-in accounts, choosing Apply & save this section saves approved fields to your private program profile. Other fields and entries are preserved. Skipped sections are unchanged. Guest updates stay in the current browser session until you create an account and explicitly save your profile. A failed save keeps approved edits in the profile editor and offers a retry. The original source file is not stored by this import. Updating your private profile does not change a saved resume or publish/update a digital CV.

Optional public digital CV

A digital CV is a separate, opt-in public snapshot. Nothing is published when you sign in, save a private profile or complete an AI build. In Share a digital CV, choose sections, inspect the public preview and agree before publishing or updating. Name, professional headline, summary and selected career sections are published. Email, phone, city/state/country and LinkedIn are off by default and are published only when selected. Clearance fields, supervisor contacts, credential identifiers, private notes, purchase records, medical and VA fields, original files and resume photos are not copied into this feature. Review your own descriptions for sensitive information; automatic field exclusions cannot detect every sensitive fact in free text.

Published snapshots are stored in Firebase’s publicly readable publicProfiles collection and served through profiles.veterancareerpath.com. They include the publishing account identifier, a program-specific link identifier, timestamps and selected public content. They are separate from both private app records and public profiles created on the main website. Anyone can view, copy or redistribute the public page without an account. Search indexing is discouraged by default; you may allow indexing, but this preference is not access control. Do not treat an unlisted link as confidential. The public page itself does not send profile content to an AI service.

Profile edits do not automatically change the live CV: refresh from your profile, check the preview and publish an update to the same link. Unpublish deletes this app’s active public snapshot, with a short public-page cache refresh; downloaded copies, screenshots, social previews and search caches can persist. Deleting your private background or saved resume does not unpublish this separate snapshot. Use Unpublish in My digital CV, or contact support for removal. Account data export includes the current published snapshot. Authentication/account deletion requests should include public CV removal. Hosting providers may process access logs when anyone opens a public link.

Purchases and recovery storage

Beginning a $1.99 USD checkout requires a separate storage agreement. We first save a purchase record in private, server-owned Firebase records; no resume is needed for checkout. After Stripe verifies payment, or a completed $0 coupon checkout, the builder unlocks. When you consent to AI processing and submit your resume, we store its fixed input and application context, then its AI review and rewritten result when successful. If you use the included clarification update, we also store the answers and updated result. This lets you return after cancellation, payment, a connection failure or an AI failure without losing a purchase. Purchase records include your account ID, build ID, timestamps, status, amount, currency and Stripe session ID. Original imported files and full card details are not stored by this program.

Stripe receives purchase identifiers, price and payment information entered at Stripe-hosted checkout; our checkout request does not include resume text or service details. Stripe operates payment, fraud-prevention and financial recordkeeping under its own policies. Card entry occurs on Stripe, not our resume form. A failed AI build remains retryable under the same purchase. Formatting and downloading that result do not require another purchase.

Retention, export and deletion

Saved backgrounds, saved resumes, and checkout build inputs/results remain until deleted or an account-deletion request is processed. Use Delete on individual resume versions, application records or saved cover letters, Delete saved workspace background, or Delete data beside a purchase to remove that input, clarification answers, AI result and generated career kit from the active database. Deleting a purchase's data does not cancel payment, issue a refund or make the deleted result recoverable. Minimal transaction identifiers and accounting/security records may be retained for legal obligations and dispute handling. In-progress builds must finish before their data can be deleted.

Download my account data exports the program background, up to 50 saved editor versions, up to 100 application records and up to 100 saved cover letters. Purchase history displays up to 50 builds; each can reopen its original input and result for download. For a full export, older records, correction, deletion of all program data, or identity/account deletion, contact support. Tell us whether you mean only this resume program or your shared Firebase identity, since deleting the identity affects other website tools. The main Privacy Policy's deletion timelines and backup-retention provisions apply. Deleting data does not erase downloaded files, other devices, financial records or provider-retained processing records.

Cookies, logs and security

See the Cookie Policy for essential account storage, opt-in device drafts, payment cookies and separate main-site preferences. Application code does not log resume bodies, AI results, credentials or identity tokens. Hosting and providers may retain operational connection/security logs. Guided profile setup

Guided setup is optional. If you enable private account saving, using Next, Skip or Finish later stores your current career profile and setup progress in the program’s Firebase account records. Without that choice, entries remain in the current tab until you explicitly save them; closing the tab can lose unsaved work. Skipping a section does not erase information you entered. Optional resume photos and design settings

Photo-enabled civilian templates can include a JPG or PNG image. The app resizes the photo on your device. It is never sent for AI review. A photo stays in this tab unless you enable Remember my draft or explicitly save a resume version; those choices store the photo with your design settings. Federal exports exclude photos. Remove photo clears it from the current draft; delete older saved versions separately to remove their copies.

This does not grant access to Gmail, Google Drive or other Google files. The app does not copy your Google profile photo into your resume. Linking Google to a guest account preserves that identity and its purchases. Switching to an existing account does not automatically merge guest work or purchases and requires confirmation. Profile photos and professional links

You may save a cropped JPEG profile photo and portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn or other professional links privately with your profile. Photos are resized on your device and are never included in AI resume requests. Each photo or link has a separate digital CV sharing choice, off by default. Publishing your CV after enabling a choice makes that photo or link public at your existing CV URL. Unchecking it and publishing again removes it from the active page after the cache refresh; copies made by other people cannot be recalled.

Application imports and calculator

Posting text and PDF, Word, TXT or CSV files are read on your device without AI. Suggestions do not change saved applications until you approve and save them. CSV imports store the selected application fields privately in your app account; original files are not stored. The app does not sign in to job boards or automatically submit applications. VA calculator percentages and dependent selections stay in the current tab and are not saved to your profile, sent to AI or added to resumes. Support links open your phone or the selected external provider.

Career matching and interest checks

AI resume assessments may produce civilian career ideas using your submitted resume and verified details. These results are stored privately with the purchased resume for recovery. They are exploration guidance, not hiring predictions, credential verification, or employment eligibility decisions.

The optional VCP Career Interest Check runs on your device. Its answers are not sent to AI or added to your public Virtual CV. If you explicitly choose Save progress / results, the app stores your answers privately in your program account. You can delete them from the Career explorer or include them in an account export.
