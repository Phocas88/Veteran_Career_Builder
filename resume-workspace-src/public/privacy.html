VETERAN CAREER PATH

Resume data & AI notice

Effective October 18, 2026. This notice supplements Veteran Career Path’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Veteran Career Path is operated by StreamSync Solutions. This workspace is for adults 18 and older.

What you choose to share

You can import a PDF, Word (.docx), or text resume, type a draft, or enter military and civilian background details. File extraction runs in your browser; the original file is not uploaded by this workspace. Your draft, edits and export files can be used without a cloud save. Do not enter SSNs, birth dates, account credentials, classified information, medical information, VA claim records, or sensitive military documents. Include only the contact information you want on a resume.

AI processing is a separate choice

When you agree to AI processing and request a review, the resume text, target role, pasted job announcement, application type, and verified additional details are sent to our review service. The builder on veterancareerpath.com uses the existing authenticated VCP service and Anthropic’s API. The standalone mobile-resume-review-app.vercel.app preview uses OpenAI’s API with store:false. Service providers process this content under their own API data and retention policies. This is not a promise of zero retention or that data never leaves your device. Canceling a review stops the browser from using the result; it cannot retract content already sent to the provider.

AI may misinterpret military terms or invent claims. Check duties, titles, dates, numbers, education, certifications, clearance status and eligibility before applying edits or exporting. Specialty codes and ranks are not proof of civilian qualifications. No ATS score, hiring outcome, government acceptance, veteran preference or eligibility is guaranteed.

Optional cloud storage using the website’s Firebase project

Account authentication and saved data use Google Firebase in the existing veteran-career-builder project. Signing in alone does not save the draft or service background. After you check the storage agreement and choose Save, the selected resume or workspace background is stored under your authenticated account. Saved resumes include their text, title, application context and formatting choices. The workspace background includes the service, contact, education and skills details you entered. AI critiques and original uploaded files are not saved by this feature.

Firestore access rules restrict these profile and resume documents to their authenticated owner. They are not automatically published to a public veteran profile. Authorized service operators and infrastructure providers may access data as necessary to operate, secure and support the service. Security rules do not prevent someone using your unlocked account or shared device from viewing your information. Never share passwords or session tokens.

Retention, access, correction and deletion

Saved account data remains until you delete it or request account deletion, subject to the main Privacy Policy and applicable legal obligations. Open a saved resume, correct it, then save a new version; delete older versions you no longer need. The Delete button removes the chosen saved resume from the active database. Delete saved workspace background removes only the background stored by this feature. Your original website profile and other saved tools are separate.

Download my account data exports your profile and up to the 50 most recent saved resumes. For a complete export, full account deletion, other data rights or support, use VCP’s contact page. Requests are handled under the main Privacy Policy. Deleting a saved item or signing out does not erase downloaded copies, other devices, provider-retained processing records or backups. The main policy describes deletion timelines and backup retention; this feature does not promise immediate backup deletion.

Cookies and browser storage

This workspace does not load analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, social embeds or marketing forms. The TikTok link opens the external profile only when you select it. Other VCP pages and external services have their own preferences and policies.

Essential browser storage supports your sign-in session, the existing AI entitlement session, security, and acknowledgment of the storage notice. By default, new sign-ins in this workspace use browser-session persistence. Keep me signed in enables persistent Firebase account storage until you sign out or clear it. An existing sign-in from the main website may already be persistent.

Remember my draft is off by default. If enabled, resume text and application context are saved in this browser’s local storage. Disable it or clear the draft to remove that local copy. We do not enable Firestore offline disk caching in this workspace. Cloud saves require their own agreement and explicit Save action. Cookie & storage preferences on the builder explains these controls. Signing out clears the workspace and its device draft; downloaded copies remain yours.

Operational data and safeguards

The AI service verifies account and entitlement tokens. It keeps account-specific request counters to limit abuse; these counters contain no resume text. Hosting and providers may retain standard connection and security logs under their policies. The workspace’s application code does not log resume bodies, credentials or AI responses. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.
