Experience Bullets — Before & After

The core rewrite
Before
"Responsible for accountability of assigned weapons and equipment during two deployments."
After
"Maintained 100% accountability of $4.2M in mission-critical assets — weapons, optics, comms, and vehicles — across 6 cyclic inventories and two overseas rotations, with zero loss or discrepancy over 8 years."
Fix   Added dollar value, scope, cadence (6 inventories), and a results metric (zero loss). "Responsible for" became a measurable outcome.
Before
"Drew and turned in supplies and ammo for platoon operations."
After
"Managed end-to-end requisition, issue, and reconciliation of supplies for a 40-person unit, forecasting demand and coordinating resupply to austere locations with 98% on-time fulfillment."
Fix   Reframed a routine duty as a supply-chain process (requisition → fulfillment), added unit size and an on-time KPI employers recognize.
Before
"Led a squad of soldiers and trained them on their jobs."
After
"Supervised, scheduled, and developed a 9-person team; built and ran a training program that qualified 100% of members on time and cut task completion time by ~20%."
Fix   "Led and trained" is invisible to ATS. Added headcount, supervisory verbs (scheduled, developed), and a process-improvement result.
Before
"Used the Army supply computer system to order parts."
After
"Operated GCSS-Army (SAP-based ERP) daily to place orders, track inventory, and run status reports — directly transferable to SAP, Oracle, and warehouse management systems (WMS)."
Fix   Names the real system and explicitly maps it to SAP/Oracle/WMS so both the recruiter and the ATS credit you with ERP experience.
